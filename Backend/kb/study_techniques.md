# Study Techniques and Habits
Active recall: close the book and write what you remember. Spaced repetition: review after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week and 1 month.
Pomodoro: 25 minutes focused study, 5 minutes break; after four rounds take a longer break.
Visual learners: use diagrams, mind maps and videos. Auditory learners: explain topics aloud or join study groups. Reading/writing learners: make notes and summaries. Kinesthetic learners: build projects and practice hands-on.
Weak topics: schedule them first when energy is highest, study in small steps and test yourself weekly.
Reduce procrastination: set a tiny first task, remove phone distractions and track a daily streak.
